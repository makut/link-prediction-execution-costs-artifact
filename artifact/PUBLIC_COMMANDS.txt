never included in source/evidence assets or required on a laptop. Set absolute
paths ARTIFACT (code artifact), REPRO_ROOT (its reproduction/ folder), ASSETS,
DATA, RESULTS, CPU_PY, PENCIL_PY and shared TIMING_COORDINATION. The two supplied

prepared23 builder below executes when invoked and has no --execute flag.

lpp_public_systems_evidence_v1 from its whole-hash-verified external archive;
extract the separate trusted archive once on the compute host. No new timing
is performed by the recorded verification commands below.
